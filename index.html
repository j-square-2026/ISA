About the Institute

A single discipline, practised in two languages.

We bring together laboratories and studios under one roof, so that a question about light, matter, sound or perception can be pursued with both a measuring instrument and an open hand.

Our fellows work across materials science, bio-inspired design, computation, sound, and the visual arts. Every project is documented openly and shared through public lectures, exhibitions and peer-reviewed publications.

Research & Art

Three ways we work.

Each programme pairs a scientific question with an artistic method, and ends in something the public can see, hear or touch.

Materials

Matter & Light

Quantum materials, metamaterials and pigment chemistry studied alongside printmakers and glass artists — turning laboratory discovery into objects you can hold.

Biological design

Living Systems

Bio-inspired engineering and ecology meet sculpture and installation. Fellows prototype materials grown rather than manufactured.

Read about the group →
Sound & perception

Signal & Sense

Psychoacoustics, data sonification and interactive media. We study how humans read signals, and build instruments that let them hear the result.
